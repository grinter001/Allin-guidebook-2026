Captain's Agreements
  • 1
    The Coin Toss — Flipped in Miami on August 5th. The winner chooses the advantage in odd or even sessions.
    Odd = Sessions 1 (Best Ball) · 3 (Individuals) · 5 (Scramble)
    Even = Sessions 2 (High & Low) · 4 (Best Ball) · 6 (Individuals)
  • 2
    Picking Order — Before each session, both Captains and their teams meet to agree on who faces who in that session.
    Pair sessions — The team with the advantage seeds their pair (two players) first. The opposing team responds with their two players. The opposing team then seeds their next pair, and the team with the advantage responds. Order: AA / BB / BB / AA / AA / BB.

    Individual sessions — The team without the advantage seeds their player first, then the other team responds, alternating throughout: B / A / B / A / B / A / B / A / B / A / B / A.

    Pair constraint — Teams cannot pair the same two players together in two consecutive pair sessions. (e.g., if Caco & Negro played together in Session 2, they cannot be paired again in Session 5.)

    Individual constraint — A specific head-to-head matchup cannot repeat between Session 3 and Session 6. (e.g., if Henry faced JT in Session 3, that match cannot happen again in Session 6.)
  • 3
    Picking Order Advantage
    Pair sessions: team that tees off first holds the advantage.
    Individual sessions: team that responds holds the advantage.
  • 4
    Tee Boxes — Fixed for all Ryder Cup rounds. No flexibility. Polla rounds are exempt.
  • 5
    No Tiebreakers — In the event of a 12–12 draw, the Cup stays with Team CAP, the defending champions.

If any pairs finish Leg 2 tied, the tie is resolved on the Putting Course. Tied pairs face each other in Gross Score Putting — best ball, hole by hole, sudden death — until a winner is decided.

Section 03 — The Venues

Venue 01 · August 7

Belvedere Golf Club

Ryder Cup — Best Ball
📸 Photos
Source: Golf.com / Brian Walters Photography · Holes 16, 15 & 9
Week 1 starts here. ⛳ Belvedere Golf Club — Charlevoix, Michigan. Opened in 1927, designed by Scottish master William Watson on a narrow strip of land nestled between Lake Michigan and Lake Charlevoix. Often called the best course you've never heard of, Belvedere is a study in timeless design: dramatic green complexes, a ridge that winds through the property, and a set of par-3s that demand your full attention from the first tee. Our opening session puts Team CAP against Team CUP in the classic Ryder Cup format — Best Ball — where each pair plays their best ball score against the other pair. Find your partner. Pick your spots. The journey begins. 🏔️
Venue 02 · August 8

Bay Harbor Golf Club

High & Low · Barracas Cup Leg 1
📸 Photos
Source: bayharborgolf.com · Links #7 aerial, Quarry holes, Preserve
27 holes. One legendary day. 🌊 Bay Harbor Golf Club — Bay Harbor, Michigan. Arthur Hills took a decommissioned shale quarry on the shores of Lake Michigan and transformed it into one of the most dramatic golf experiences in the country. 40-foot gorges, stone cliffs, a waterfall, and panoramic lake views at every turn. Our longest day covers all three nines: 🏌️ High & Low (18 holes) — Links + Quarry: the highest and lowest handicaps paired together, where windswept dunes meet the rugged beauty of the old quarry. 🏆 Barracas Cup — Leg 1 — Individual net stroke play on the Preserve 9. Your score here sets the standings for Leg 2. Play with purpose. Often called the "Pebble Beach of the Midwest." On this day, we'll believe it.
Venue 03 · August 9

Arthur Hills at The Highlands

Ryder Cup — Individuals
📸 Photos
Source: highlandsharborsprings.com · Hole #13, holes #4 & #5
Every man for himself. 🎯 Arthur Hills at The Highlands — Harbor Springs, Michigan. TheGrint Top 100 · #97. Par 73 · 6,352 yards from the Orange tees. Ranked among the finest courses in Michigan, this Arthur Hills masterpiece winds through deep pine forests, wetlands, and dramatic elevation changes that will test every club in the bag. The 13th hole — BOYNE Golf's Everest — plays downhill from the top of a ski slope. The fairway is wide enough to land a plane. The view is something else entirely. Our third Ryder Cup session is all Individual match play. Six singles matches, six points on the board. This is where reputations are made. Handle your business. 🤝

A short round inspired by the classics.

Doon Brae Short Course at The Highlands — Harbor Springs, Michigan. Nine par-3 holes designed by Raymond Hearn, set along the hillside that doubles as a ski slope in winter. Doon means going down into a valley; Brae means hillside in Scotland — an apt name for a course routed along the slope.

Venue 05 · August 10

The Heather at The Highlands

Ryder Cup — Best Ball
📸 Photos
Source: highlandsharborsprings.com · Heather course holes 18 & 5
The legend. ⛳ The Heather at The Highlands — Harbor Springs, Michigan. Designed by Robert Trent Jones Sr. in 1966, the very first BOYNE Golf course is still one of the finest championship layouts in the United States. 2018 Michigan Course of the Year. 2019 National Course of the Year. The pedigree speaks for itself. The Heather winds through classic Northern Michigan evergreens and doglegs before finishing with one of the game's great closing holes — a long par-4 over water that will test your nerve right when you need it most. Our fourth Ryder Cup session plays Best Ball. Three two-man partnerships per side, each match worth one point. Momentum swings here. Read your partner. Par is your friend. Bogey is a gift. 🌲
Venue 06 · August 11

Arcadia Bluffs — The South Course

Ryder Cup — Scramble · Polla
📸 Photos
Source: arcadiabluffs.com · South Course · TheGrint Top 100 #73
Complexity veiled by simplicity. 🤍 The South Course at Arcadia Bluffs — Bear Lake, Michigan. TheGrint Top 100 · #73. White tees · Par 72 · 6,380 yards. Inspired by the golden age classics — no trees, wide fairways, firm and fast, and greens averaging over 9,400 square feet. The wind is always a factor. The strategy is never obvious. Our fifth Ryder Cup session brings the Scramble format — four players, best drive, all play from there. Big hitters rejoice. Short game decides it. And the Polla tees off here too: a $20 buy-in individual net stroke play side bet. Lowest net takes the pot. 12 players. $240. One winner. A day of decisions — out on one of the best young courses in America. 😏
Venue 07 · August 12–13

Arcadia Bluffs — The Bluffs Course

Ryder Cup Finals — Individuals · Polla
📸 Photos
Source: arcadiabluffs.com · The Bluffs Course · TheGrint Top 100 #12
The Final Stand. 🏆 Arcadia Bluffs — Arcadia, Michigan. TheGrint Top 100 · #12. White tees · Par 72 · 6,389 yards. Wind-swept native grasses. Sod-walled bunkers. Panoramic views of Lake Michigan from every hole on the property. Some of the most dramatic golf real estate in the United States. This is the final Ryder Cup session: Individual match play — The Finals. Six singles matches. Six points. The Cup is decided here. 12.5 points wins. A draw means the Cup stays with Team CAP — the defending champions. Team CUP came to take it home. The second Polla runs alongside: 12 players, $20 each, $240 pot. Individual net stroke play. Best ball on the best course of the week. And on August 13 morning, the Bluffs Course hosts the Polla Long at 7:58am — the last competitive round of the trip, before the Champions Dinner. The Cup awaits. ⚔️
